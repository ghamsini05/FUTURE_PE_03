## Local SEO Best Practices

* Mention each city naturally throughout content.
* Use city names in headings where appropriate.
* Include location-specific FAQs.
* Add local business schema.
* Use geo-targeted image alt text.
* Create city-specific landing pages.
* Build internal links between related city content.
